        When it gets hard

        Specific situations, specific responses — decided now, not negotiated in the moment.

        Power / no internet

        • Prayer and Bible reading with journal — needs nothing
        • Prayer walk — needs nothing
        • Draft outreach messages offline, send when back

        No motivation

        • Open the laptop, write one line of code — that's the whole ask
        • Do 50% of the day rather than zero — zero is what actually breaks a streak

        You miss a day

        • Don't miss two — that's the real threshold, not the first miss
        • Name what caused it and one fix — then move on, no spiral

        Clients go quiet

        • Double outreach that day, don't wait it out
        • Offer an upgrade or referral deal to an existing happy client
        • Church and community network — trust beats cold outreach here
