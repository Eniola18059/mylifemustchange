Section 02

Fasting &
Eating Protocol

16-Hour Daily Fast

Fast 16 hours every day counted from your last meal the night before. Your eating window is 8 hours only. If you eat last at 8pm, you do not eat again until 12pm the next day. During the fast: water, black coffee, plain herbal tea only — nothing with calories.

→

→

→

→

→
Repeat

What to Eat (12pm–8pm)

  • High protein every meal: eggs, chicken, fish (Titus/sardines), beans, Greek yoghurt
  • Controlled carbs: oats, sweet potato, brown rice, plantain — not every meal
  • Vegetables: as much as you want, always included
  • Target: 2,000–2,400 calories in your eating window
  • 3 litres of water total across the day

Never Touch These

  • Zero soda — permanently, not temporarily
  • Zero fruit juice — liquid sugar, no nutritional benefit
  • Zero junk food — no fried snacks, no fast food
  • Zero added sugar in drinks or food
  • Allowed drinks: water, zobo (no sugar), herbal tea, coconut water, tiger nut milk

Section 04

Skincare &
Grooming System

Consistency beats expensive products. 60–90 days of these routines transforms how you look, how you feel, and how every room receives you. This is discipline made visible.

☀️ Morning — Every Single Day

  • Gentle face wash — CeraVe or quality local brand. 30 seconds, rinse cold.
  • Moisturiser with SPF 30+ — Lagos sun causes dark spots without this. Every morning.
  • Lip balm. Deodorant. Signature cologne. Small details that signal self-respect.

🌙 Night — Every Single Day

  • Same gentle face cleanser. Remove oil, sweat, Lagos pollution. Never skip this.
  • Night moisturiser — skin repairs during sleep. Feed it. After 90 days add Vitamin C serum.
  • 3 litres of water daily — clears skin faster than any cream in Lagos heat.

Monthly Rhythm

Every Week

  • Sunday: church, teach one person something valuable
  • Sunday night journal: weight, income, 3 wins, 1 thing to fix honestly

1st of Every Month

  • Weigh + measure waist, chest, arms + take one progress photo
  • Review income and savings — hitting 25–30% saved?
  • Rate discipline 1–10. Below 6: simplify and recommit immediately
  • Count projects and clients delivered that month
  • Celebrate one win. Read this plan from the top. Adjust nothing hastily.
When It Gets Hard

Power / Internet Out

  • Prayer and Bible reading with journal — always available offline
  • Prayer walk — 10,000 steps, requires absolutely nothing
  • Draft outreach messages to send when connection returns
  • Workout — completely offline, zero excuses ever

Motivation is Zero

  • 5-minute rule: open the laptop and write one line of code only
  • Scroll up and read the anti-vision section on this page
  • Visualise your life unchanged at 30. Feel it. Then move.
  • Do 50% of the day — never do zero. Zero kills habits permanently.

You Miss a Day

  • Do not miss two. The second miss is where habits die for good.
  • No guilt spiral — acknowledge it and execute 50% today
  • Write what caused the miss and one system fix to prevent it repeating
  • Tomorrow is a clean start. Every morning is a new Day 1.

Clients are Slow

  • Double outreach to 20+ messages that day immediately
  • Offer existing happy clients an upgrade or referral deal
  • Use slow periods to sharpen your skills and build portfolio
  • Church and community — trust always beats cold outreach in Naija
