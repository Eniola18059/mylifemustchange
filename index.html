    Anti-procrastination system

    This week's non-negotiables. Check them as they happen — they reset when you tap "New week."
      90-day lock: no redesigning this plan before month 3. Five roadmap rebuilds already happened — rebuilding is itself a form of avoidance. This version runs as-is first.
      If a study block is missed: it's made up the same day at reduced length — not carried as guilt into next week.

      1st of every month

      Five minutes, same checklist, every month — this is what actually catches drift early.
      • Weigh in + one honest look, not a judgment session
      • Review income against the target on the Money tab
      • Count study hours actually completed vs. planned
      • Name one win, one honest miss, one system fix — then close the review, don't dwell

      When it gets hard

      Specific situations, specific responses — decided now, not negotiated in the moment.

      Power / no internet

      • Prayer and Bible reading with journal — needs nothing
      • Prayer walk — needs nothing
      • Draft outreach messages offline, send when back

      No motivation

      • Open the laptop, write one line of code — that's the whole ask
      • Do 50% of the day rather than zero — zero is what actually breaks a streak

      You miss a day

      • Don't miss two — that's the real threshold, not the first miss
      • Name what caused it and one fix — then move on, no spiral

      Clients go quiet

      • Double outreach that day, don't wait it out
      • Offer an upgrade or referral deal to an existing happy client
      • Church and community network — trust beats cold outreach here
